## Founder perspective

I'm the **Founder & CEO of Dyagnosys Health**. My work spans product and technology strategy, AI/software architecture, prototyping, partnerships, business development, and commercialization.

I approach engineering from a founder's perspective: **working code is the beginning, not the end.** The goal is to turn technology into a product that solves a real problem and has a reason to exist in the market.

## Current focus

- Building practical **AI agents and autonomous workflows**.

- Developing **trading and market-microstructure software**.
- Turning prototypes into simpler, measurable, commercially useful products.
